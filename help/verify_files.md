# Verify your game files for corruption
This guide will show you how to check your game files for corruption on Steam and Xbox/Microsoft Store/Gamepass.

## Steam

1. Right click CSL2 in game list or press gear icon on game page
2. Click on `Properties`
3. Navigate to `Installed Files`
4. Click `Verify integrity of game files`

## XBox App / Microsoft Store / Gamepass

1. Navigate to CSL2.
2. Click the three dot menu
3. Click `Manage`
4. Navigate to `Files`
5. Click `Verify and repair`
