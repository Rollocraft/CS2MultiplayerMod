# Player camera navigation

Open the multiplayer panel and find **Player cameras**. **Go to** moves your view to that player's camera, including its angle and zoom. **Follow** keeps your view smoothly aligned as they move. Both hosts and clients can use these controls.

Choose **Stop following**, press Escape, or use the camera controls to regain control. Following also stops during a world sync, on disconnect, when switching camera modes, or when the target has not sent a position for three seconds. Typing in a text field does not cancel following.
