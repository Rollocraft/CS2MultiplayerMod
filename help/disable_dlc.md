# Disabling DLC
Learn how to disable DLC on Steam and on XBox/Microsoft Store/Gamepass.

For CS2MultiplayerMod, you only need to disable DLC that the other players do not have. All players should have the same DLCs activated (or no DLC activated).

## Steam

1. Right click CSL2 in game list or press gear icon on game page
2. Click on `Properties`
3. Navigate to `DLC`
4. Disable the relevant DLC

## XBox App / Microsoft Store / Gamepass

In the Xbox app:
1. Open `My Library`
2. Right-click CSL2, click `Manage`
3. Remove DLC and add-ons

In the Settings app:
1. Open the Windows Settings.
2. Navigate to `Apps > Installed Apps`
3. Navigate to CSL2. Click on `Advanced Options` in the three dot menu next to the app.
4. Remove DLC and add-ons.

---

The CS1 Treasure Hunt DLC cannot be disabled, so the mod no longer compares it — you can join
each other whether or not you own Cities: Skylines 1.
