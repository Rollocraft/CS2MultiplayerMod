# Playing via Direct Connection

If you are playing on your local network or able to set up Port Forwarding for internet play, it is recommended to use **Direct Connection**. Use **Steam Relay** for easy use. 

## Playing through the internet
For playing with friends that are not connected to your local network, you need to setup TCP port forwarding. Only do this with people you **absolutely trust**.

- [Written Tutorial](help/forwarding.md) (recommended)
- [Video Tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WOZQppVNGvA)

**Instructions:**

1. Set up port forwarding (Default port: `25001`) like described in the tutorial above.
2. Find out your IP Address ([Link](https://api.ipify.org/))
3. Share the IP Address and the port you forwarded with your friends.

**Issues? Check [Troubleshooting](/help/forwarding_troubleshoot.md).**

---

## Playing on your local network
This is suitable if all players are in the same household or connected to the same network.

- Players connect to the local IP address of your PC.
- Find out your local IP address by running `ipconfig` in command line. Look for "IPv4 Address". On Linux: Use `ifconfig´ or `ip addr` and look for your ethernet adapter (usually ´eth0´).
